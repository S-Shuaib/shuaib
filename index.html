The reporting question

What happens after the technology is announced?

That question sits underneath much of my work. I am interested in the distance between an innovation on paper and the people who have to use it, regulate it, secure it, pay for it or live with its consequences.

04 / About

I report on technology because technology is increasingly where public life happens.

I am a Nigerian technology journalist and digital policy analyst whose work sits at the intersection of journalism, technology and public policy.

I write about AI, cybersecurity, digital regulation, emerging technologies, digital infrastructure and the ways digital systems affect institutions and communities. My technical background in software development and cybersecurity helps me engage with technology beyond the surface. My journalism work helps me translate that understanding into accessible stories for a broad audience.

I also work in digital training and media intelligence, giving me practical experience with digital tools, newsroom systems and the challenge of turning complex information into useful public knowledge.
